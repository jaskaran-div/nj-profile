My story

I’m a strategist working on how professionally competent capability is formed across finance education, markets and institutions.

I structure cross-border academic–commercial partnerships that integrate regulated, practitioner-led financial trading training and Global Capital Markets qualifications into higher education institutional ecosystems.

Grounded in regulatory and academic finance experience, I bring calibrated judgment to financial sector development: strengthening market credibility, aligning risk readiness with governance standards, and safeguarding institutional integrity through internal and external quality assurance and periods of transformation.
